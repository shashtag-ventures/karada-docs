Modern engineering teams build and operate MCP servers collaboratively. Karada provides **Team Workspaces** with granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure developers, DevOps engineers, and security teams have appropriate access to projects, deployments, secrets, and billing.

---

## Workspaces Architecture

A Workspace is an isolated multi-tenant environment containing your projects, custom plugins, and gateway endpoints:

- **Multi-Workspace Membership**: Join multiple team or enterprise workspaces with a single user login.
- **Context Switcher**: Rapidly switch between personal, staging, and production workspaces from the top navigation bar.
- **Centralized Billing**: Subscription tiers and resource limits apply at the workspace level rather than per seat.

---

## Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Karada enforces strict RBAC to prevent unauthorized secret exposure or unapproved production deployments:

<ResponseField name='Owner' type='Highest Privilege'>
  Owners hold full administrative control. They manage payment methods and subscriptions, invite or remove team members, configure gateway domains, and delete workspaces or projects.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name='Admin' type='High Privilege'>
  Admins can create projects, generate Auto-MCP servers, configure plugins, manage runtime environment variables, and trigger deployments. They cannot modify billing or delete the workspace.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name='Member' type='Standard Privilege'>
  Members can inspect projects, review generated tool catalogs, and query usage telemetry. They cannot expose raw secret values or trigger unreviewed production rollouts.
</ResponseField>

## Inviting Team Members

To add collaborators to your workspace:

1. Open your **Workspace Settings**.
2. Select the **Members** tab.
3. Click **Invite Member**.
4. Enter your collaborator's email and assign their role (**Admin** or **Member**).
5. The invitee receives a secure link to join your workspace immediately.
