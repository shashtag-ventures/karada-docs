title: "Guide: Account Settings"
description: "Manage your profile, authentication, and account preferences."
---

Your account settings control your personal identity on the Karada platform. Access them by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner and selecting **Account Settings**.

## Profile

### Avatar

Your avatar displays next to your name across the platform — in team lists, deployment logs, and comments. Click on your existing avatar to upload a new one from your device.

<Note>
  An avatar remains optional but we strongly recommend adding one to improve team collaboration.
</Note>

### Display Name

Set a display name that other team members see. This can be your full name or any alias you prefer.

- Maximum **32 characters**.
- Click **Save** to apply changes.

### Email

Karada uses your primary email for:

- **Login authentication**
- **Account-related notifications** (deployment alerts, team invitations)

Your email displays with a **Primary** badge and you cannot change it through the UI. Contact support if you need to update it.

---

## Authentication

Karada handles authentication securely via third-party identity providers (such as Google or GitHub).

- **No passwords to manage**: Your identity provider handles authentication.
- **Automatic session management**: Karada securely manages your session access.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Enable 2FA on your identity provider for an additional layer of security on your Karada account.

---

## Billing

The **Billing** section allows workspace owners to manage their subscription plan and payment methods. Billing always associates with a **Workspace**, not an individual account.

To view or update billing:

1. Navigate to your **Account Settings**.
2. Select the **Billing** tab in the sidebar.

---

## Danger Zone

At the bottom of your account settings, you will find the **Delete Account** section.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is **permanent and irreversible**. This action will:
  - Remove your personal workspace and all its projects
  - Revoke your access to any team workspaces
  - Delete all associated deployment history

  You must confirm this action before the deletion proceeds.
</Warning>
