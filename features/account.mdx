Your account preferences control your personal profile, Single Sign-On (SSO) identity links, and workspace associations on the Karada platform. Access them anytime by clicking your avatar in the upper right and selecting **Account Settings**.

---

## Profile Settings

### Avatar & Identity

Your avatar appears across deployment activity logs, project audit trails, and team member directories. Click your avatar to upload a custom image.

### Display Name & Primary Email

- **Display Name**: The human-readable alias visible to teammates across workspaces (up to 32 characters).
- **Primary Email**: Used for account authentication, deployment alerts, and invitation links. Managed via your OAuth SSO identity provider.

---

## Secure Authentication & SSO

Karada prioritizes infrastructure security by integrating with verified identity providers (GitHub, Google):

- **Zero Passwords**: Eliminates password reuse vulnerabilities.
- **Hardware MFA Support**: Seamlessly inherits multi-factor authentication (MFA / 2FA) enforced by your identity provider.
- **Session Tokens**: Protected by HTTP-only, secure cookies with strict expiration lifetimes.

---

## Workspace Billing Access

Subscription management is associated with **Workspaces** rather than individual accounts. Workspace owners can access invoices, upgrade tiers, and update credit cards under **Account Settings → Billing**.

---

## Decommissioning & Account Deletion

The **Danger Zone** allows you to permanently terminate your account:

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent. Deleting an account dissolves your personal workspace, stops active MCP server containers, purges environment secrets, and detaches you from team workspaces.
</Warning>
